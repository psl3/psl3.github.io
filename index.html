Clean, purposeful mobile experiences for Android

Five-0

A strategic tile-placement game. Challenge the device by creating rows that sum to multiples of five. Simple to learn, difficult to master.

NTVisited

This app compliments the National Trust's formal app and providing a method of ticking off which properties visited and the date visited which can be viewed in a list form or on a map. Additional brief information, and navigation is provided.

Dove Towers

This app provides a list of all Bell Ringing Towers and map locations enabling the user to record when they have visited/rang at the tower. Additional information such as number of bells and practice night is provided where available.

CallChanges

This app supports learning to lead 'Call Changes' in Bell ringing. Select call sequences and learn required calls or practice ringing virtually, seeing changes in real time and hearing bells play your calls.
