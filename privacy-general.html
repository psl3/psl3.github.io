Privacy Policy

General Policy for SavvyFixes Applications

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications (the "Applications") created by SavvyFixes (the "Service Provider"). Our apps are designed to provide utility and information with a focus on user privacy.

Data Collection and Use

The Applications do not personally collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). There is no requirement for user registration or account creation.

  • Update Verification: The Application performs a simple check with a read-only cloud database to verify the current version number and any mandatory update messages. This ensures you are using the most secure and up-to-date version of the service.
  • Location Data: Information regarding locations and maps is coded into the app; this is local and is updated when the app receives an update. Where property images are shown, these are received read-only from the National Trust website. Their use of data is governed by the National Trust Privacy Policy.
  • Local Storage: Your personal checklists and "visited" markers are saved directly on your device. We have no access to this data.

Third-Party Access

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to outside parties. Because we do not collect user data, there is no data to share. These specific applications are ad-free and do not contain third-party marketing SDKs.

Children’s Privacy

Our Applications do not address anyone under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, parents can be assured that no personal information is being harvested.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
