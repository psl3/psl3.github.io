Privacy Policy

General Policy for SavvyFixes Applications

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile applications (the "Applications") created by SavvyFixes. Our apps are designed to provide utility and information with a strict focus on user privacy.

Data Collection and Use

The Applications do not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). No user registration or account creation is required.

  • Update Verification: The Applications perform a simple check with a read-only cloud database (Backendless) to verify current version numbers and display mandatory update messages.
  • Content & Property Updates: To ensure information is accurate without requiring a full app store update, the Applications may download updated informational data (text files) from our secure cloud storage to your device's internal storage.
  • Local Assets & Images: Core assets (such as audio files) are stored locally. Where property or informational images are shown, these are received as read-only assets directly from relevant organizational websites (e.g., National Trust).
  • Local User Data: Your personal checklists, "visited" markers, and app settings are saved exclusively on your device. We do not transmit or have any access to your personal visit history.

External Links

The Applications contain links to external websites (such as the National Trust or Dove websites) to provide further information. Please be aware that we do not have control over these external sites. Once you leave the Application, you are subject to the privacy policies of those third-party websites.

Third-Party Access

We do not sell, trade, or transfer any information to outside parties. Because we do not collect user data, there is no data to share. These applications are ad-free and do not contain third-party marketing SDKs.

Children’s Privacy

Our Applications do not address anyone under the age of 13 and we do not knowingly collect any information from children. Since no data is collected from any user, parents can be assured that no personal information is being harvested.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
